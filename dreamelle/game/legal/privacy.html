Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Dreamelle is an original life-simulation game. This policy explains what information the game and its website (dreamelle.app) collect, why, and the choices you have. Dreamelle is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

What we collect

  • Game progress (your character, looks and progress) is saved on your own device. We do not need an account to play.
  • A random device ID created by the game. It lets us deliver your Founder's Pass automatically and understand how the game is played. It is not linked to your name.
  • Gameplay events such as "started the game", "finished Day 1" or "opened checkout", the game version, the language and which ad or link brought you to the game (UTM parameters). We use these to improve the game and test new versions.
  • Purchase information. Payments are processed by Hotmart (or by Apple / Google when you buy inside an app store). We receive the transaction code, the email used at checkout and the purchase status so we can unlock your Founder's Pass. We never see or store your card details.
  • Advertising measurement. The website uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads (for example, page views and checkout clicks). Meta may use cookies or similar technologies for this purpose.

How we use it

To run the game, deliver and restore purchases, provide support, measure and improve the game and our ads, prevent fraud, and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run Dreamelle: Hotmart (payments), Supabase and Vercel (hosting and database), Meta (ad measurement) and, in the apps, Apple or Google (app distribution and in-app purchases). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Gameplay events are kept for up to 24 months. Purchase records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your local game data at any time in Settings → Reset progress.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data linked to your device ID or purchase email by writing to us (see Contact). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA or Brazil's LGPD) you may have additional rights, including to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority.
  • You can limit ad tracking in your browser or device settings.

Children

Dreamelle is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
