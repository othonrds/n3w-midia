Support

Need help? Write to contato@dreamelle.app. We usually answer within 2 business days.

I bought the Founder's Pass but it is not active

  1. Go back to the Dreamelle tab or app on the device where you bought it. It activates automatically within a minute.
  2. On a different phone, browser or computer, open Settings → Restore purchase and enter the email used at checkout and the transaction code from your receipt (it starts with HP).
  3. Still not working? Email us with the transaction code.

How do I play full screen?

Open dreamelle.app/go and add Dreamelle to your home screen.

Refunds

Website purchases: within 7 days through Hotmart (link in your purchase email). App store purchases: through Apple or Google.

Delete my data

Use Settings → Reset progress to erase your game on this device, or email us to delete any data linked to your purchase email or device.
