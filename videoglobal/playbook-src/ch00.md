# Start here

*Hi, I'm Maggy. I'm AI-made — and that's exactly why I can teach you this.*

I'm Maggy Lane, and I'm not a person. My face, my photos and my videos are generated with the same tools you're about to learn. Every day I see the tells that give AI away — the waxy skin, the extra finger, the face that changes between two shots — because I have to fix them in myself before anything gets posted.

This playbook is the system I use. It's not a course and it's not theory. It's a working studio you can run from a laptop or a phone: which tool to open for which job, how to write prompts like a film director, how to keep one face consistent, and how to finish photos and videos so nobody's first thought is "that's AI."

## What's inside

**Part 1 — Foundations**

1. **Your AI studio** — which tool for which job, free options first, and a one-page tool cost map.
2. **Prompting like a director** — prompt anatomy, camera and lens words, light, film looks and structured prompts.
3. **One face, every shot** — consistent characters, reference sheets and keeping a face stable from photo to video.

**Part 2 — Photos**

4. **Pro portraits from selfies** — headshots, editorial and lifestyle portraits, and restoring old family photos.
5. **Product photos without a photoshoot** — catalog shots, styled scenes and ad grids from one phone photo.

**Part 3 — Video**

6. **From still to motion** — image-to-video, camera moves, short clips and multi-shot sequences.
7. **Six repeatable viral formats + the edit** — six formats you can turn into a series, and how to cut, caption and export.

**Part 4 — Quality**

8. **Make it not look AI** — the six tells and their exact fixes, a troubleshooting table and a pre-post checklist.

## How to use this book

- **Read Chapters 1 and 2 first.** Everything else builds on them. It takes about an hour.
- **Then jump to what you need.** Want profile photos? Chapter 4. Product shots? Chapter 5. Reels? Chapters 6 and 7.
- **Copy the prompts.** Every gray box marked as a prompt is ready to paste. Swap the parts in [BRACKETS] for your own details. In the online version, tap **Copy** on any prompt.
- **Do the "Try it now" at the end of each chapter.** Fifteen minutes of making beats an hour of reading.
- **Keep Chapter 8 open** before you post anything.

## Three promises I can keep (and one I won't make)

1. You'll know which tool to open for each job, and which free option to try first.
2. You'll have copy-ready prompts for portraits, products and short videos.
3. You'll know how to spot and fix the six things that make AI content look fake.

What I won't promise: income, followers or going viral. Those depend on you, your niche and a lot of luck. What I can give you is the skill and the system.

> **Heads up:** AI tools change every month. Every tool in this book was checked as of late 2026, but names, features and prices move. When something has changed, the principles in each chapter still apply — that's why I teach jobs first and tools second.

> **Ethics:** Only use faces you have permission to use — your own, or someone who said yes. Never recreate real people, celebrities, brands or characters you don't own. Label AI content wherever the platform asks for it. Everything in this book follows those rules, and your work should too.

Now open Chapter 1 and set up your studio.
